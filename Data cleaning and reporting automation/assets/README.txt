Marvel Avengers Hanging Animations - Custom Asset Guide
=========================================================

How to Use Custom Images:
-------------------------
You can add your own custom PNG, JPG, or SVG images to this folder (assets/) to replace procedural vector shapes or add custom Marvel characters!

1. Save your transparent PNG image in this directory (e.g. assets/cap_shield_custom.png).
2. The image will be automatically loaded and attached to strings, chains, or energy tethers!
3. Recommended image dimensions: 128x128 or 256x256 pixels with transparent PNG background.

Enjoy customizing your hanging desktop animations!
